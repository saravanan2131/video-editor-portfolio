✦ My Creative Process

From Brief to Final Export

A structured approach that ensures quality and consistency in every project.

Step 01

Understand the Brief

Grasp the project goals, target audience, platform requirements, and creative direction before touching any footage.

Step 02

Collect & Organise Assets

Gather all raw footage, audio files, graphics, and reference materials. Organise everything for an efficient workflow.

Step 03

Rough Cut

Assemble the initial timeline — placing clips, establishing the flow, and building the story structure.

Step 04

Transitions & Effects

Add smooth transitions, motion effects, text overlays, and visual elements that enhance the viewing experience.

Step 05

Audio & Colour

Synchronise music and sound effects, apply basic colour correction, and ensure visual consistency throughout.

Step 06

Final Review

Watch the complete edit multiple times. Check for timing, errors, and overall impact before the final export.

Step 07

Export & Deliver

Render in the correct format, resolution, and specs for the target platform. Deliver the final polished product.
